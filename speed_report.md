## Analysis

### Why Speedup Is Not Higher
1. **Coverage is 40.6%** — only ~1 in 4 positions fires a draft. The rest fall back to greedy (1 token/pass).
2. **Draft accuracy is 61.4%** — ~39% of proposed tokens are rejected. Each rejection costs a truncate + restart.
3. **767 no-rule passes** — positions where no pattern matches at all.
4. **Match rate 75%** — 5 prompts diverge from greedy, causing early termination of draft chains.

### What Helps Most
- **k=8 vs k=6**: +0.015x time speedup (more tokens per accept)
- **Truncate vs seq-bonus**: +0.087x time speedup (cheaper rejection recovery)
- **Loose thresholds**: +0.122x time speedup vs strict (more rules fire)

### Remaining Limiters
- **Corpus mismatches**: 5 prompts don't match greedy. Fixing corpus gaps would push to ~1.50-1.55x.
- **Low coverage**: Need more corpus examples or live mining to reach 35-45%.
- **Reject recovery**: Even truncate mode has KV cache manipulation cost.

### Recommendation
To reach 1.50x+ wall time speedup:
1. Fix the 5 corpus mismatches (add exact greedy outputs to corpus)
2. Improve live n-gram accuracy (currently 184 fires but mixed results)
3. Test k=10-12 on boilerplate-heavy prompts
4. Add prompt-specific rule packs (linked-list, tree, sort templates)
